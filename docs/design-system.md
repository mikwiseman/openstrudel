# OpenStrudel design system

A warm, quiet place where small strudel helpers do the work. The home shows what they did today; the conversation is one tap away.

This is the contract for the Mac app, the iPhone and iPad app, and the local web UI. Every value here is deliberate. If a screen needs a value that is not listed, add it here first.

## Principles

1. **Space first.** Start with too much room and take it away, never the other way round. 22 at the edges of a phone, nothing touching its neighbour, no rules between rows: distance separates.
2. **Glass is for controls, never for content.** Liquid Glass belongs to what floats over the page: the capsule at the top of a page, the composer, the round buttons, the Mac's sidebar and toolbar. Messages, cards and tiles are never glass. Where the system draws glass itself (bars, sheets, menus), it is left to.
3. **Tiles are the content's face.** Today's work is shown as squircles with a soft shadow, a small label and one big thing: a picture, a tinted paper, or a number.
4. **Ink and one signal.** Black ink on warm paper, inverted in the dark. The primary action is an ink capsule with a canvas-coloured label. One periwinkle signal marks what is live or unseen: working, new, the arrow of a view. Amber is for what went wrong. No other hue belongs to the interface; the colour comes from the characters and their tones.
5. **The characters carry the personality**, big and on a soft circle in their own tone. Everything around them stays plain.
6. **Native before custom.** System sidebar, toolbar, sheets, grouped forms in Settings, alerts and menus. Custom drawing only where the product is its own: the home, the transcript, the pages of an employee.
7. **Shared edges.** Text in a column starts on one line. Control edges line up with the column edges.

## Type

SF for the interface, New York for what is read as a publication, SF Mono for code. Hebrew falls back to SF Hebrew by itself. No custom fonts.

| Role | Mac | iOS (Dynamic Type) |
|---|---|---|
| Display: the wordmark, welcome and empty-state titles, big numbers | SF Semibold 26, tracking −0.6 | `.title` semibold, tracking −0.6 to −0.9 with size |
| Headline: a story, a tile's title, our view | New York Medium (`design: .serif`) 17–34 | the matching text style, serif, medium |
| Title: sheet and pane titles inside content | `.title2` semibold | `.title3` semibold |
| Reading: messages, the composer, summaries | SF 15, `lineSpacing(3)` | `.body` (17), `lineSpacing(4)` |
| Interface: rows, buttons, settings | `.body` (13); a team row's name 14.5 semibold | `.body`; a team row's name `.body` semibold, its preview `.subheadline` |
| Label: over a tile or a group | SF Semibold 12, sentence case | `.caption` semibold, sentence case |
| Meta: times, quiet lines, captions | `.subheadline` (11) or 12.5 | `.footnote` |
| Code | SF Mono 13, `lineSpacing(4)` | `.subheadline.monospaced()`, `lineSpacing(5)` |

- Nothing is set in capitals. A label is a short sentence-case phrase in `SecondaryText`.
- Weights: Regular, Medium, Semibold.
- Markdown headings stay small: H1 and H2 are 17 Semibold, H3 to H6 are 15 Semibold. 24 above a heading, 8 below.
- Tabular figures for times, counts, percentages and prices.
- Tiles cap Dynamic Type at xxLarge, and the wordmark and the glass capsules at accessibility1, like widgets and bars; pages follow the reader's size all the way. A tile's label falls back to a shorter form («News · 5») before it is cut.
- Web stacks are unchanged: `--font-sans`, `--font-display` (`ui-rounded`), `--font-mono`, weights 400 / 510 / 590.

## Space and shape

- Layout gaps: **4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.** The phone's margin is **22**; the Mac sidebar's is 14; the Mac window edge is 20. Line heights are not spacing.
- An icon and its label: 8. A character and its text: 14 (11 in the Mac sidebar). Groups on a page: 38. Turns in a transcript: 32. A time cluster: 48.
- Radii (always `.continuous`): **8** dense controls; **12** code blocks and tables; **20–22** notices and file chips; **24** message bubbles, with **8** at the corner that points at the author; **26** the composer when it grows; **28** cards in the transcript and pictures in a story; **36** a tile (30 on the Mac), 40 for a page's lead tile. Capsules for buttons, chips and the bars that float.
- **A tile** is 150 square on a phone and 126 on the Mac, 12 (10) apart, padding 16 (14), a two-layer shadow (10 % black, radius 13, y 10; 5 %, radius 2, y 1) and a 1-pt white edge at 18 % (a `SeparatorColor` stroke with increased contrast). Its label sits top-left; the big thing fills the rest. Three kinds: **picture** (the story's image; the label reads over a darkened top, and the title stands on its own scrim that starts just above its first line (`pictureScrim`), so no line of the picture runs through the words and the picture shows in all the room they leave), **paper** (the employee's tone, or ink for the next run), **data** (white, a ring or a number). A title has at most four lines and steps its size down rather than being cut (`FittedTitle`); on paper a title that opens with a name before a colon («Лодка: аренда катеров…») shows the name large in serif and the rest small under it. Desk labels read alike: when one long label («Startups · 3 новых») does not fit, every desk's is the short one («News · 5»). A tile presses to 0.97 and springs back.
- **Characters:** 54 in a phone's team row, 40 in the Mac sidebar, 30 in a title capsule, 24 beside the latest message, 84–112 on welcome and empty pages. Always on a circle of the character's tone (`AgentAppearance.tint`: saturation 17 %, brightness 97.5 % in light; 28 %, 27 % in dark).

## Colour

Warm paper, ink, one signal. Values are light / dark / light increased contrast / dark increased contrast, all in the asset catalogue. Ink, secondary, warning and signal text pass 4.5 : 1 on the canvas in every appearance; a signal-coloured word uses `SignalText`, a step darker than the dot in light.

| `SeparatorColor` | `#E2E0DA` / `#34322F` / `#8C8A85` / `#7A7773` | table rules, the quote rule, increased-contrast strokes. Never a rule between rows |
| `AccentColor` | `#3D3C39` / `#8C8A85` / `#1F1E1C` / `#A8A6A0` | the system accent: selection, focus, switches, default buttons |

| `WarningText` | `#A85A14` / `#E5B078` / `#7A3F0A` / `#F6D3A3` | what went wrong: a failed run, an error, a lost connection |

- `NewsStatusFill` and `NewsStatusText` are gone: the signal marks what is new, words say the rest.
- Primary text is the system `.primary`. Success is a word or a symbol, never a colour alone. Colour is never the only signal of state.
- Increased contrast: tiles, cards, code blocks, file chips and tables add a 1-pt `SeparatorColor` stroke. Reduce Transparency and Reduce Motion are the system's: glass turns solid, a zoom becomes a fade, a press dims instead of shrinking.
- The canvas is the main window only. Sheets, the Settings window, popovers, menus and grouped forms keep system backgrounds.
- Web only: `--control-border` `#86808C` / `#7C7781` / `#6F6A74` / `#8F8A94` for inputs (≥ 3:1).

## Characters

- The main assistant is the brand bun from the app icon without its tile (`Agent-main`), so every row in the sidebar is a character. The app icon tile stays for the Dock, About and the menu bar.
- **The helper.** One character sits at the "now" edge of every conversation: 24 pt at the leading text edge, 12 below the last transcript item. Idle, it is still. While the employee works, it proofs like rising dough (scale x 1.03, y 0.95, anchored at the bottom, ease-in-out 0.9 s, repeating) beside "{Name} is working…" in meta style. When a reply lands, it plops once (scale y 0.86 over 90 ms, then a spring back, response 0.35, damping 0.55); iOS adds a soft haptic. The loop pauses when the window is inactive. Reduce Motion: no scale, a 150 ms fade of the words only.
- **Offline.** When an employee's device is offline, its character renders desaturated at 60 % opacity in the sidebar, toolbar and helper, and its preview reads "Offline". It returns over 0.4 s with the character spring. Grey means out of reach and nothing else: an employee whose run failed keeps its colours, and the small warning mark and the words say what happened.

## Mac structure

- **Window:** a `NavigationSplitView` whose sidebar is **the home** (`HomeView`, 300 to 420 wide): the bun and the wordmark, what waits for the owner, the papers, the shelf of tiles, the team, and a search capsule at the bottom («Найти», ⌘F) under a hard scroll edge, so rows end at it instead of showing through. The detail column is the place that is open, the chat by default, in its own `NavigationStack`. View lists the chat and the team in the home's order, ⌘1 to ⌘9 for the first nine and the rest by name, then under a rule the pages («Ждёт вас», the polls, Progress, Statistics), so a long team never pushes a page off the menu. `.windowToolbarStyle(.unified(showsTitle: false))`. The canvas is set with `.containerBackground(AppTheme.canvas, for: .window)`. Minimum window 760 × 560. One layout direction per window, fixed at launch from the chosen language. The language menu appears on the welcome screens only.
- **What waits comes first** (`HomeNeeds`, `NeedCardView` in `NeedsYouViews.swift`; the model is `NeedsYou.swift`). The home leads with the cards that wait for the owner, from every Home: «Ждёт вас · 3» in the signal colour, then on a phone the first card whole, with the edges of the next two under it, and beside an open page the first three as rows and «Ещё 2». A card is one decision: a small character and who raised it, the Home's name when it is not the chat's own, how long it has waited or its day in `WarningText` («завтра»); what is true as a serif headline; why now in one quiet sentence; then the step as capsules, the main one in ink. Words that will leave are never sent from the home: there the main button reads «Прочитать и отправить» and opens the page, where the whole text stands on a quiet fill with who receives it and through what, and «Изменить» makes it editable before it goes. A choice that takes words («Другое», «Новая дата») opens one line to write them in, on the page. An answered card lifts away, the next rises on a spring (response 0.35, damping 0.8) with a soft haptic, and with none left one calm serif sentence stays, «Ничего не ждёт вас», with the last decision under it («Отправлено: Команда · 5 мин») or how much was done today. Nothing moves while it is calm. A Home out of reach claims nothing.
- **Where the cards come from.** A Home that keeps cards sends them (`GET /v1/cards`, answered with `POST /v1/cards/{id}/answer`; kinds send, answer, due, connect, failed) and its buttons are shown in its own words. Until a Home has that, it is read for what it can already tell: a question waiting in the chat is a card, answered in place when it is one short choice and otherwise in the chat. On every Home an employee whose runs fail is one card with the reason as its headline, since when under it, «Открыть» and «Завтра» (set aside on this device until the morning), unless that Home has already raised a card about it. A question comes first, then decisions with the longest-waiting on top, then what failed. What only a Home's owner may read (its cards, a failure's reason) is forgotten as soon as the Home refuses this connection.
- **The page «Ждёт вас»** (`InsightsPage`) lists every card whole, then «Решено» (what was decided in the last day, on any Home, here or in Telegram, with by whom when it was not this app), then the recent results of the chat's Home for reading. It replaced «Что важно».
- **The papers** stand under what waits and above the shelf: each News desk's paper as a wide tile (see «News reader»). **The shelf** (`HomeShelf`, `Shelf` in `Team.swift`) holds answers and shows only what is real, in this order: the latest day of a chat's daily polls (`CheckInTile`), «Дальше» (the nearest run of any employee in sight), then the answers of the last day that came with a picture. There are no gauges on it: what needs the owner is the lead card, and the limit is a figure at the foot of the home. Up to three tiles stand in one row; more make two rows that scroll sideways and stop on a tile. With nothing to show there is no shelf.
- **A team row** (`TeamRow`, the same on the phone): the character on its tone, the name in semibold with, on the same line at the trailing edge, the time of the latest answer in `FaintText` («сейчас», «5 мин», «2 ч», «вчера», a weekday, a date; it ticks each minute) and a signal dot for something this device has not had on screen; under them one line that has the row's whole width. The line and the character say one thing, in this order: «Нет связи» with the character in grey; «Работает…» in the signal colour with the character proofing inside a signal ring; «Не получилось» in `WarningText` with a small mark on the character, which keeps its colours, and after a colon the reason in the run's own first line when the Home tells its owner («Не получилось: закончился лимит модели», `TeamFailures`); otherwise the latest answer as one plain line. The context menu pins, renames, hides on this device, or moves to another device. Beside an open place the open row wears a soft plate.
- **The team is one list across every Home** (`teamList`, `TeamArrangement` in `Team.swift`). The chat's row is first. Then the **pinned** employees, whichever Home they live on, in a steady order by name, each with a small faint pin beside its time. Then everyone else: whoever has a card waiting (the row wears the signal dot), then whoever is working, then the latest answer first, so the list reads like a list of conversations. A Home is never a section. With several Homes it is a quiet word after the name on every row that does not live on the chat's own Home, and a filter over the list: one line of words above the rows, «Все» and each Home's name, the chosen one in ink and the rest in `SecondaryText`; a Home out of reach says so beside its name, and a Home with something new that the filter hides carries a small signal dot. The choice is kept on the device. With one Home chosen its own assistant is listed under the chat. With one Home connected there is no line and no word. Whoever has said nothing for two weeks and has nothing going on (not working, not failing, nothing unread, no card) waits under one line, «Давно молчат» with their faces (`TeamFaces`), which a tap opens for as long as the home is in sight; nothing is hidden for good, and search finds everyone. The home ends with its quiet doors: «Статистика» with the fullest limit as a figure («лимит недели 68 %», in `WarningText` from 90 %), and who is hidden on this device. ⌘2… follow what is in sight: the pinned, then the list.
- **The lists stay current while the app runs.** On the Mac the library itself asks every Home for its employees (`keepCataloguesCurrent`): every two seconds with a window in sight, every half a minute with none, so the menu-bar item and a window opened later show the team as it is, never as it was when the window closed.
- **A page** wears a capsule at the top with whose page it is (`TitleCapsule`: the character and the name; it opens the details, also ⌘I) and one "…" button with everything else the bar used to offer. On the Mac the capsule sits in the toolbar's centre. Under the bar the paper closes over what scrolls up (`topFade`, `paperTop`): content dissolves before it reaches the capsules, with the system's soft scroll edge and never a rule, on the phone and under the Mac toolbar alike. A phone's home has no bar and closes the same way under the clock, so it keeps no rule there with Reduce Transparency on either.
- **An employee's page** (`ReaderView`): one quiet line under the capsule that never wraps (at accessibility text sizes it takes a second line rather than being cut), the rhythm and its next run («Каждый день в 06:45 · следующий сегодня»; no date, each entry carries its own; «и ещё 1» only when it fits), which opens the details; a run that failed stays pinned under it in `WarningText` with since when and why. Then its work as a calm feed: each result as open text under its time, pictures as tiles, no bubbles. At the bottom a glass capsule, «Спросить про <имя>», takes its latest words to the one composer. A News desk opens its reader instead.
- **Chat:** the chosen Home's main assistant and the one composer of the app. The transcript column is centred, max width **560**, under the toolbar and over the composer. Every unresolved send of the Home stays in this transcript with its destination, retry and delete.
- **Readers:** a Telegram group, an archive, another Home's assistant, found through search. They scroll and are read; they never compose. «Обсудить в чате» in a message's or a story's menu attaches it to the chat's draft and opens the chat.
- **Settings window (⌘,)** and **sheets** keep the system's styling: grouped forms, toolbar buttons for cancel and confirm, alerts for confirmations. No cards or hand-made dividers there.

## iOS structure

The home is the first screen and there is no tab bar. On a phone `HomeView` is the root of one `NavigationStack`: the header (bun, wordmark, and the account button that opens Settings), the shelf, the team, and a dock at the bottom: a glass capsule shaped like the composer («Сообщение», `homeComposer`) and a round glass search button (`openChats`). The capsule is not a second composer: it opens the chat with the keyboard up, and the chat grows out of it. A card, a row or a tile opens its page with a zoom from itself and goes back into it. The header is followed by what waits for the owner (one whole card, see the Mac structure), then the shelf, then the team as one list. Statistics opens from the foot of the home and from Settings; the limits in full and the first steps (Progress) are a step from Statistics. On an iPad at regular width the home is the sidebar, as on the Mac.

The launch screen is the canvas colour, so the paper is there from the first frame.

In the chat the bar is a glass back button, the title capsule and "…" («Переписки», the assistant's details, Settings). The keyboard belongs to the chat while the chat is in front: a page or a sheet over it takes the keyboard down, and it comes up only once the page has really arrived (UIKit's word that the transition is over), so the composer never ends up under it. Settings opens in its sheet with the existing identifiers. When the Home's sign-in is lost, the notice with its one action is on the home as well as in the chat. All text uses text styles, so Dynamic Type works to AX5; hit targets are 44.

The docks at the bottom of the home and of a page draw their glass through UIKit (`DockGlass`): SwiftUI's own glass effect crashed on iOS 27.0 when such a page came back into sight while the chat's keyboard was still leaving (`TeamUITests.testTheChatGoesBackIntoItsCapsuleWithTheKeyboardUp`).

## Transcript

- **The person (one-to-one):** a trailing ink bubble (`UserBubbleColor`, words in `CanvasColor`), radius 24 with the corner that points at the author at 8, padding 16 × 9, max width 75 % of the column (420).
- **The employee:** open text on the canvas, leading-aligned, full column width. No bubble, no avatar per message, no author label.
- **Multi-author conversations** (imported history, Telegram groups): other people's messages are leading-aligned on `SurfaceColor`, radius 24, with "Anna · 12:04" above in meta style.
- **Cards** (a question, an approval, a notice): a white tile (`TileColor`, radius 28, padding 18, the tile's shadow at 70 %) with capsule buttons. A yes-or-no decision leads with its yes in ink; other choices are even `SurfaceColor` capsules. An action's arguments read as quiet rows, the name in secondary and the value in primary, when they are a flat object; only a nested payload stays as JSON, in small mono (`InteractionDetail`).
- **Rhythm:** 32 between turns, 12 between consecutive messages from the same side, 12 between paragraphs, 6 between list items, 24 above and 8 below a heading, 48 above a time cluster header and 16 below it (none above the first).
- **Time:** a centred cluster header in `FaintText` at the meta size when a new day starts or after a gap of 30 minutes or more ("Today, 12:02"; day only when a message has no time). Exact time: `.help` on Mac and `accessibilityValue` everywhere. Schedule and notice lines keep their time. Queued, failed and error states stay visible under their message.
- **Markdown:** lists use a fixed marker column so bullets and right-aligned tabular numbers share one text edge. Quotes: 2-pt `SeparatorColor` rule, 12 gap, secondary text. Links: ink (`AccentText`), underlined at rest (underline in `SecondaryText`; on the web the underline turns solid ink on hover); only `https`, `http` and `mailto` stay links, natively and on the web. Inline code: SF Mono 13 on `SurfaceColor`. Code blocks: `SurfaceColor`, radius 12, padding 12, copy button on hover (Mac) or always (iOS). Tables: `SurfaceColor` header, hairline rules, a column whose cells are all numbers is trailing-aligned with tabular figures; horizontal scroll when wider than the column.
- **Files:** chips as small white tiles, radius 20, padding 12, icon 20, name 13 Medium, size in meta. Images radius 24 with the tile's shadow, max 320 × 240. A web picture named in a reply loads only when the person asks, because its address can carry data out: natively a 240 × 180 surface with its host and "Download image", on the web a link. Attachments load on sight.
- **Right to left:** each block aligns by the direction of its first strong letter, so English in the Hebrew interface stays left-aligned and Hebrew in the English interface is right-aligned. The person's bubbles and the helper follow the direction of the employee's latest reply (the interface's when there is none), so the bubbles always sit opposite the employee's text edge: on the right beside English replies, on the left beside Hebrew ones. Directional symbols use `forward` and `backward` variants.

## Composer

One capsule of Liquid Glass: "+", the words, the microphone. With one line it is a capsule (48 high on the Mac, 54 on a phone); it grows to 8 lines with radius 26 and then scrolls. Its edges are the transcript column's edges. The send button is the only filled thing: an ink circle with a canvas-coloured arrow that appears with the first word or file and becomes Stop while the employee answers. With text while the employee answers, the steer / after-the-reply menu sits before the microphone. The model and speed control is a quiet row inside the glass, shown while the composer has the keyboard or something in it. The queue tray, attachment chips and the context chip (`draftContext`) sit inside the glass above the text. Placeholder: «Сообщение», everywhere. Drop target: a 2-pt ink stroke at the same radius. No glass inside the composer.

Under a floating bar at the bottom the paper rises softly (`dockFade`), so what scrolls beneath fades instead of showing through the glass.

## Empty states and onboarding

The character large on its tone circle, one sentence, at most one capsule button. Empty conversation: the character at 112, the name in display, the role in at most two lines within 360; the composer is the action. An employee without results: its character, «<Имя> ещё ничего не прислал» and nothing to press. Welcome, consent, connecting, sign-in and offline (`WelcomePage`): the character at 112, a display title set tight, the body centred under it while it is short reading (leading-aligned past eight lines), one ink capsule; secondary actions are plain text buttons. The words are optically centred at 40 % of the room they have, 22 from the edges of a phone. On a phone the actions stand at the bottom, in the thumb's reach; on the Mac, the iPad and at the largest text sizes they follow the words.

## Statistics

How the connected Homes were used, by day (thirty days) or by week (twelve, from Monday), in one column 640 wide (`StatisticsView.swift`). It reads every connected Home at once, and «Все устройства» or one Home narrows it. It opens from the limit's tile on the home, from Settings on a phone and from View › Statistics on the Mac.

- **Tiles first**, the same squares as the home's shelf (`DataShelf`): the chosen day's or week's requests on an ink tile twice as wide as the others (the number set tight, `numericText`, under its sentence-case label «Запросы сегодня», with the fair comparison «Вчера к этому часу: 253»); the fullest limit as a ring when the page was opened from the home; the cost at API prices and the tokens when engines reported them.
- **Bars.** One per period from the first request on: at most 24 wide, 4 rounded at the top, 148 high on the Mac and 132 on iOS. The shown bar is ink; the rest `SecondaryText` at 30 %; a period with no requests is a 2-high stub. One reference value top right. Every bar is a button with its date and value.
- **Below**, 48 apart and without rules: each Home's share as a 4-high ink capsule on a `SeparatorColor` track; «Где работали», «Расписания» and «Модели» as open rows under sentence-case labels, the top five and «Показать все · N»; where requests came from and how they ended as two meta lines; notes in meta.
- No colour carries meaning: Homes are named, never coloured. A state without numbers is a symbol, a display title, one line and at most one action (`statisticsState-<code>`).
- **«Ждёт вас»** and **Progress** (`ActivityViews.swift`) are the same kind of page: an open column, groups 38 apart under sentence-case labels. «Ждёт вас» is described with the home (Mac structure). Progress opens with tiles (what was done in seven days, the limit's ring, the active days), then the week's counts as rows and limits as white cards; it opens from the foot of Statistics («Лимиты и использование»). The bun's sentence and the first steps with their signal ticks are shown until all three steps are taken, and not after.

## Daily polls

A chat that is asked the same questions every day (calm, focus, energy and mood from 1 to 10) is a place behind its own tile (`CheckInViews.swift`; the arithmetic lives in `CheckIns.swift`). The polls of one chat are one board: what their names share («Exhale · calm», «Exhale · focus») names the board, the rest names each measure. The numbers are the people's own answers. A missing answer is missing, never zero, and different measures are never folded into one figure.

- **The tile** on the home: the board's name, the latest answered day as levels (one pair of bars per measure under its initial) and the day in a quiet word («сегодня», «вчера»).
- **People without colour.** The first person is filled ink, the second an ink ring, anyone after them quiet: the same mark stands beside a name, on a chart's points and in the tile's bars. No hue tells two people apart.
- **The page**, top to bottom in the column of the pages behind tiles: the **day** as its title («Сегодня», «Вчера», «Понедельник, 9 ноября»: the latest day anyone answered) and the **people side by side** as white tiles as tall as their words (each measure and its number; «Серия: 16» under them, «Пока без ответа» for someone who has not answered today); one under another at accessibility text sizes. **«Что заметно»**: at most three plain rows, the strongest first: an answer two points or more from the person's own usual, a week that moved a point or more against the week before, two people's weeks two points or more apart, a measure that sits two points or more under the person's own other measures this week («За неделю 4, остальное около 6,3»). Every comparison needs three answers on each side; with nothing to say the section is absent. **«По дням»**: one small chart per measure on a shared day axis that starts at the first answer and shows fourteen days at least: a line through the days each person answered, points only where there is an answer, a dotted rule where the last seven days begin, the scale's ends as its only labels. **«Неделя к прошлой»**: each measure's mean over the last seven days per person with its change against the seven before («−2,2»). Then the chat the polls live in.
- **One day at a time.** A finger held on any chart, or the pointer over it on a Mac, moves the whole page to that day: the title, the people's tiles, every chart's head and rule, with one soft tick for each day crossed. Letting go returns to the latest day.
- A streak counts the days a poll was asked on: a day off (the Saturday of a Sunday-to-Friday schedule) neither adds to it nor breaks it.

## News reader

An employee with stories is a desk, and its stories are read as a paper (`NewsViews.swift`, `NewsWeek.swift`, `NewsPosts.swift`, `NewsHome.swift`; the arithmetic lives in `NewsDesk.swift`). There is nothing to type into the paper itself: «Спросить про News» takes the lead to the chat, and a story's dock has two ways on, «Спросить» and the ink capsule «Сделать пост».

- **The way in** is the desk's paper at the top of the home (`NewsPaperShelf`): a wide tile, 184 high on a phone and 148 in the Mac sidebar, with the day's lead as its headline, the lead's picture under the scrim or the desk's paper, and «News · 5 новых» as its label. With no picture the headline is the tile's face: a step larger, on four lines. One paper fills the row; of several, the next one shows its edge. It is there from the first moment: the device keeps the last answer of the Home in the app's own caches folder (the stories, not their pictures), so the paper opens before the Home answers and reads without a connection. A desk on a Home that is still connecting is not called offline.
- **The front page**, top to bottom in a 680 column: the **day as its title** and one **quiet line** that does not wrap («Выпуск 07:04 · 21 история · радар 14:35»); three quiet capsules, **«Неделя», «Посты · 2», «Найти»**; the desk's own sentence on the day when its edition has one; **the lead** as a large tile (radius 40) with section and status as its label and the headline in serif on it, then its first paragraph, the desk's view after a signal arrow, and the sources as small capsules; **«Новое с 14:05 · 3»** in the signal colour, only for what was reported since the reader last left today's paper, as airy rows, the five newest and «Показать все · N»; **«Главное»**, the next four stories as rows with two lines of summary and the story's picture small beside them when it has one; **«Разбор»**, the points the headlines missed, as ink tiles (one fills the column, more stand in a row that scrolls); **the rest under their sections** as rows of a quiet line (time and status) and a serif headline; **«Выпуски»**, the earlier days as a shelf of plain tiles with the day, its lead and its count.
- **A lead with no picture is set in words**, never left with a hole where a picture would be. The tile is the desk's paper and exactly as tall as what it holds: the figure the story stands on, 64 semibold on a phone and 76 on the Mac, with what it measures under it, then the headline; a story with no figure gets the headline alone, a step larger (31 on a phone, 38 on the Mac, up to five lines). While a named picture is on its way the tile keeps the picture's height, so the page does not jump; a picture too small to be a face (under 480 × 240) stays on the story's own page.
- **The order** of a day's stories (`NewsDesk.paper`): what the desk sent with sound, then the edition as its desk ranked it, then the cards, then the rest of the radar, the newest first among equals. Every story stands in one place on the page. Before today's first story the latest day's paper stands in, and the quiet line says so.
- **The quiet line** is exactly one of: the day's count and the radar's last check · «News проверяет источники…» · «Радар не работает с 10:05: причина» in `WarningText` · «News на паузе с 8 ноября» · «Сегодняшнего выпуска нет. Ниже выпуск за 8 ноября.» A Home out of reach says so instead.
- **Seen**, **arrivals** («2 новых ↑» in a glass pill) and **language** work as before: a story counts as seen after 1.5 seconds at rest on screen or when opened; new stories wait behind the pill; labels follow the stories' language. The device also keeps which stories were opened and on which days the paper was read.
- **A story** pushes over its page in a 620 column: its picture (radius 28, the tile's shadow), a quiet line, the headline in serif 34, the summary, the view after the signal arrow, **the figures** that carry it as square tiles (the first in ink: the value large, what it measures under it), source links as capsules; then «Что произошло», «Доказательства», «Развитие», and **«Раньше по теме»**: the names the story is about as capsules, each a page of everything the desk wrote about that name, and the earlier stories about the same names. «↻» in the signal colour marks a development. On the Mac the story takes the keyboard: Esc goes back, J and K or ← and → move through the page's stories.
- **A post** (`NewsPostSheet`, a system sheet): the stories it is written from, with «Добавить историю» for up to five; Telegram or LinkedIn as a segmented control; while the Home writes, the desk's character proofing and one sentence; then the draft on a white tile, to read and edit **the way the post will look**: a bold title is bold and a link is its words in the signal colour, underlined; no Markdown marks show (`NewsPostText`). The Home keeps Markdown, and an untouched draft goes back to it letter for letter. Under the draft: the desk's note (what the channel already said, what could not be checked), a field «Что изменить?» that sends the change in words, and at the bottom the ink capsule «Скопировать», a share button and «Ещё вариант». **«Скопировать» puts rich text on the clipboard** (RTF, then HTML) with a plain version that has no marks and keeps each link's address in brackets. One exception: a Telegram draft copied on a Mac carries no plain version, because Telegram for Mac pastes the plain one whenever it is there and drops the bold and the links. Share hands over the same three, the plain one first. The draft is written and kept on the desk's Home; nothing publishes it. «Посты» lists every draft.
- **The week** (`NewsWeekPage`): tiles first (stories this week on ink against the week before, what was opened, the drafts, the run of days with the paper), then «Что движется» as each section's share (a 4-high ink capsule on a `SeparatorColor` track, its count, and «+6» with an arrow against the week before: a shape and words, never colour alone), «Компании и люди» from the names the desk gives its stories, «Истории с продолжением», and the drafts.
- **A story's picture** (`NewsStory.image`) loads over https, or over http only from the Home's own address. Nothing is kept for it before it loads; it fades in, and one that does not load stays out. The tile on the home and the page share it once loaded (`StoryPictures`).
- Headlines use the system serif (New York Medium). Reading text stays SF. Labels are sentence case.

## Motion

A page grows out of the tile or the row that was pressed and goes back into it (`navigationTransition(.zoom)` on iOS); the chat grows out of the home's capsule or a page's «Спросить про…» capsule with the keyboard following. Tiles and rows answer a press at once (0.97, then a spring: response 0.35, damping 0.7). Numbers change with `numericText`. Layout changes use `.smooth(duration: 0.25)`. Nothing loops except a working character. Reduce Motion: fades and dims only.

## Web UI

Mirror the Mac layout and tokens at 1 pt = 1 px: CSS custom properties for every colour in light, dark and `prefers-contrast: more`; the same sidebar (56-px rows, 36 avatars, 12 gaps, 12-px inner margin), transcript, helper and composer with the same generous spacing; the page fits the viewport exactly (`100dvh`, no clipped composer or sidebar footer); the same type scale (meta raised to the 12 px floor). Markdown is rendered by a DOM-only renderer (never `innerHTML` for message content): raw HTML is text, only `https`, `http` and `mailto` links, images become links. Each message is one `<article>`. Follow the latest message only when the reader is near the bottom. SVG icons, no text glyphs. Form controls inherit the font. Logical properties and `dir="auto"` for right to left. `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency` respected. Modals: title, content, a bottom bar with Cancel first and one primary action last. Return in a modal's text field presses its primary action. The composer keeps the leading plus (files) and the steer / after-the-reply toggle but has no microphone: the browser has no speech engine here.
