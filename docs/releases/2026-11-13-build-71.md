# Build 71 — what waits leads, a personal paper, polls from any Home

Source: `b1fe82f2c97b6ccdfd0851d96a77e730b20f53ab` (main with #147–#168).
Published on 13 November 2026 from Mik's MacBook.

Build 70 was built, signed and notarized first. Its walk through the owner's
real Homes found two faults, so it was not offered through Sparkle: after a
News story was opened beside the home, choosing another place left the story
over it (#166), and the polls page read only the Home the app is connected to,
so polls asked on another Home never showed (#167). Build 71 carries both
fixes.

## Change

- The home leads with what waits for the owner: a reply to send, a question
  to answer, a promise due, a failed employee with its reason, each with its
  buttons. The Home's cards (#158, #162) and the app's own come from every
  Home in one list.
- News reads as a personal paper: today's lead with its picture, the week,
  earlier issues, search, and a story page with its sources, what was read and
  what was not checked. A story becomes a post draft that copies with its
  formatting (#157, #163).
- One team list across every Home, with pins, a Home filter and the employees
  who have gone quiet folded away (#149).
- The daily polls have a page of their own, with each person's answers,
  streaks, what stands out and the days, from whichever Home asks them (#149,
  #167).
- On a Mac or an iPad, choosing another place beside an open story shows it at
  once; the desk opens on the story again (#166).
- The bundled runtime and the public Home archive are build 71. They carry the
  server work merged since 69: a report delivered to a group that belongs to
  another employee (#147), the PR and Sales employees' prompts (#148, #150), a
  failed scheduled message said in its chat (#151), a quiet correspondence
  watcher (#152), delivered reports recorded in their chat (#153), Home-wide
  standing rules and draft by default (#154, #161), notification routes
  (#155), compaction between turns (#156), one `OUTCOME:` line per scheduled
  result (#159), the owner's morning brief (#160), and decision cards with
  Telegram buttons (#158, #162). Android stays build 3.

## Acceptance

- The Home suite on main passes except the same 7 speech tests that fail on
  earlier releases; one run under load added a scheduler timing failure that
  passes alone. The Home packaging tests pass on the signed bundle (5 of 5).
- Mac unit tests: 445 in 43 suites on the merged head of #166. On an iOS 27.0
  simulator: `TeamUITests` (home, welcome, keyboard, iPad), `OneChatUITests`,
  `StatisticsUITests`, `NewspaperUITests.testThePaperFromTheHomeToAPost`
  (also at an accessibility text size) and the new iPad test
  `testAnotherPlaceShowsAtOnceBesideAnOpenStory`, which fails on the code
  before #166.
- Mac: Release arm64 build, runtime bundled with the pinned Node 26.7.0 (the
  app is 990 MB), Developer ID signature. App notarization
  `bd2dd383-dfa1-452c-8f85-b196fc920503` and DMG notarization
  `a05f62e1-3ef3-4b4e-898d-537869d3e10f` were accepted and stapled, and
  Gatekeeper accepted both.
- A copy of the signed app, opened hidden against the owner's real Homes:
  the home with its cards and tiles, the polls tile and page from a Home other
  than the connected one, a story and then another place (it showed at once,
  with no Back), the desk again (the story was still open), Statistics and an
  employee page.
- The DMG and the Home archive downloaded back from WaiWeb matched their
  SHA-256; the downloaded app reports 1.0 (71) and passes Gatekeeper.
- Sparkle: the one-release feed was generated with the pinned tools and the
  permanent key. The enclosure length matched the DMG, the archive and feed
  signatures verified, and the public feed is byte-identical to the signed

- Not exercised: the Mac app with its window in front and a real pointer, an
