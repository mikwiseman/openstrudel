# Build 72 — a failure's cause in the app's own words, a group's switches in one menu

Source: `563361fa19e45a94591735d8616183e50f7537c3` (main with #170–#180).
Published on 24 November 2026 from Mik's MacBook.

Main moved on to #181 and #182 after the build. They change only how the Home
releases AiGid, and are not in this build's runtime or Home archive.

A first build from `ad4d02c` was built and signed, then set aside. Its settings
sheet said the cause only for an ordinary employee; a News desk opens the same
sheet from its own page and still showed the Home's sentence (#180).

## Change

- A failing employee's row, its card and its page say the cause the Home names
  in the interface's own language: a model sign-in, a model limit, no
  connection. The Home's own sentence is shown only by a Home that names no
  cause (#176).
- The settings sheet that the failure line opens says the same words under its
  schedules, for an employee and for a desk. In a language other than Russian
  it used to show the Home's sentence half translated (#179, #180).
- A Telegram group has one menu where its pause button stood: pause, whether
  transcripts of voice and video are posted, and whether every message or only
  a mention is answered. A Mac no longer prints a title over the two reply
  choices (#176, #179).
- The French and Spanish words for a signed-out model account fit a row; they
  were cut mid-phrase in an iPad's sidebar (#179).
- The bundled runtime and the public Home archive are build 72. They carry the
  server work merged since 71: a scheduled request told its own last run
  (#170), a group's transcript switch and reply mode, with administrators heard
  in their groups (#171), a signed-out model account said once, with a cause
  code for the apps (#173), and the AiGid release requests (#172, #174, #175).
  Android stays build 3.

## Left open

- `L10n.serviceText` tries every catalogue key with a slot as a pattern, and
  the key `{0} в {1}` matches any Russian sentence with « в » in it. A Home's
  sentence shown through `UserFacingError.text` in another language can still
  come out half translated wherever the app does not have a cause to say.
- The causes are kept in memory and asked for again every five minutes or when
  the set of failing employees changes. When that request fails, on a bad link
  for instance, a row says "Did not work" until the next one succeeds.
- `ReleaseScenariosUITests` needs a repair pass before it can gate a release.
