# Build 68 — usage statistics and Settings

Source: `da7b4c97e31c4c55f914a3fce177ec6c8bc81e7c` (main `cd2bc49` plus the
version bump). Published on 9 November 2026 from Mik's MacBook.

## Change

- Statistics on Mac (sidebar, ⌘2) and iPhone (tab): requests, tokens and cost
  for each Home by day and by week, compared with the same hour yesterday or
  last week, with the busiest chats, schedules and models (#120).
- Settings opens on «Модели» (default model, reasoning, speed, Fast in direct
  chats and reserves for each Home) and adds «Расписания» (every schedule with
  an on/off switch and this week's runs, failures and cost).
- Clients is no longer part of the app.
- Also released here for the first time: sourced mobile Insights and Progress,
  employee projects in owner settings, Telegram polls in Progress, and large
  mobile layouts.
- The bundled runtime and the public Home archive are build 68, compiled from
  the release commit. They include the server work merged since 66: the
  statistics API, the GitHub tools and correspondence batches in their own
  sessions. Android stays build 3.

  issue with a separate fix.
- The Home suite on main passes 1,275 tests; the same 7 speech tests fail on
  clean main.

  Developer ID signature, and packaged tests 5 of 5 on the signed bundle. App

  `92e11c6e-273e-4460-b9d6-caaf898648e7` were accepted and stapled, and
  Gatekeeper accepted both.
- The DMG downloaded back from WaiWeb matched its SHA-256. Mounted read-only,
  the app inside passed Gatekeeper and stapler validation and reports 1.0 (68).
- Sparkle: the one-release feed was generated with the pinned tools and the
  permanent key. The enclosure length matched the DMG, the archive signature
  verified, and the public feed is byte-identical to the signed file.
- Not exercised: an installed app updating itself through Sparkle. The Mac
