# Build 69 — a new look for the Mac and iPhone app

Source: `d50de109349438f5a7082a20568f1929c033be86` (main with the redesign
#143, the team catalogue #144 and the version bump #145). Published on
11 November 2026 from Mik's MacBook.

## Change

- One home instead of tabs: today's work as a shelf of tiles (each desk's lead
  story, what matters, the next scheduled run, the week's limit, progress, an
  employee's latest picture), the team with live status, one composer and
  search. The same view is the Mac sidebar.
- Every employee has a page of its own with its rhythm from schedules and a
  plain line when its latest run failed. News desks read like a paper, with
  the story's picture when it has one.
- Chat, story pages, Statistics, Insights, Progress, welcome and sign-in
  screens are in the same visual language: a paper canvas, glass only on
  navigation and the composer, the characters on tinted circles.
- Statistics, «Что важно» and Progress open from their tiles instead of tabs.
- Fixed: the Home's own Telegram posts were taken for the owner's reply and
  shown as raw text; a glass capsule at the bottom of a page could crash the
  app on iOS 27.0 after a sheet closed (the bottom capsules now draw their
  glass through UIKit, with a regression test).
- The Home lists each employee's latest answer time, whether it is working,
  its next run, whether its latest scheduled run failed and the first picture
  of its latest answer. A Home older than this build shows its employees
  without those marks.
- The bundled runtime and the public Home archive are build 69, compiled from
  the release commit. They also carry the server work merged since 68: the
  application-written News posts and editions, the Startups desk prompts and
  the server shell update tool. Android stays build 3.

## Acceptance

- On the redesign's final head 407 Mac unit tests passed, and on an iOS 27.0
  simulator `TeamUITests` (welcome, the walk through the home, nine rounds of
  the capsule regression, iPad), `OneChatUITests`, `StatisticsUITests`, the
  pairing invitation test and both account-recovery tests passed. The walk
  through the home also passed in dark, at the largest text size, with
  increased contrast, with reduced transparency and on a small phone.
- The Home suite on the release commit passes with the same 7 speech tests
  failing as on earlier releases. A run made while Xcode was compiling failed
  six more timing tests; those files pass alone (53 of 53). The Home release
  packaging tests pass (61 of 61).
- Mac: Release arm64 build, runtime bundled with the pinned Node 26.7.0,
  Developer ID signature, and packaged tests 5 of 5 on the signed bundle. App
  notarization `b865f95f-c971-4e06-b77a-1790d072fb51` and DMG notarization
  `fbbe116a-cf7b-45cd-a8d4-2e747833db19` were accepted and stapled, and
  Gatekeeper accepted both.
- A copy of the signed app was opened in the background against the owner's
  real Homes and walked through the home, a desk, an employee page, Statistics
  and the chat without an error.
- The DMG and the Home archive downloaded back from WaiWeb matched their
  SHA-256.
- Sparkle: the one-release feed was generated with the pinned tools and the
  permanent key. The enclosure length matched the DMG, the archive signature
  and the feed signature verified, and the public feed is byte-identical to
  the signed file.
- Not exercised: an installed app updating itself through Sparkle, a physical
  iPhone, the Mac app in the foreground (hover, keyboard shortcuts, focus
  rings), right-to-left layout, and the UI suites `CheckIn`,
  `ReleaseScenarios`, `AppleReview`, `iPhoneSmoke`, `DesignAccessibility`,
  `ModelSettings`, `Extensions`, `AgentCharacters`, `EmployeeRowHitArea` and
  `StorePreview`.
